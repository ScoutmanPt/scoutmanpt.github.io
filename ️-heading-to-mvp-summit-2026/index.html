⚜️ Heading To MVP Summit 2026

MVP Summit Microsoft MVP Microsoft 365 Azure Community

20 Mar 2026 · 2 min · 288 words

I’m heading to MVP Summit 2026, and once more it already looks promising.

There is always something special about this event.

It is not just the sessions, the announcements, or the technology itself. It is the chance to be around people who care deeply about the products, the platforms, friends, and the work happening behind the scenes.

Every year, MVP Summit brings together a rare mix of energy, curiosity, expertise, and honest conversations, and that combination is hard to beat.

For me, it is always a moment to listen carefully, learn a lot, reconnect with people I admire, and come back with a fresher perspective.

These gatherings have a way of reminding us that the ecosystem is constantly moving.

Ideas evolve, priorities shift, products mature, and new possibilities start taking shape faster than we sometimes realize.

That is part of what makes the Summit so valuable.

It gives us space to step back from the daily grind and look at the bigger picture:

  • Where the platforms are going
  • What challenges customers are facing
  • What the community is seeing in the field
  • ..and how we, as MVPs, can continue helping bridge all those worlds

I am especially looking forward to the conversations.

The best moments are often not just the official ones, but the in-between moments: hallway chats, spontaneous debates, catching up with old friends, meeting new people, and exchanging the kind of insight that rarely fits inside slides.

So yes, I am heading there with an awesomeness of excitement.

And if the first signals are anything to go by, this one may once again be one of those weeks that leaves us inspired, challenged, and full of ideas for what comes next.

Let’s see what this year brings.
